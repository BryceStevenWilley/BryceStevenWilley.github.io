[HEADS UP] Clean urls are now opt-in

Internal page links now by default use the offline-viewable,
longterm-archiving-friendly "my-album/index.html" form rather
than "my-album/". If you previously used the --no-clean-urls flag for
your builds, you can stop doing so – it's now the default. If you
want to opt (back) in to clean urls, enable the "clean_urls" setting
in your global site settings/manifest.

- Documentation (in-depth): https://faircamp.org/docs/2.0.0/reference/site/index.html#site.clean_urls
- Changelog entry (shorter): https://faircamp.org/changes/2.0.0/#clean-urls-are-now-opt-in

[HEADS UP] Your 1.x catalog manifest has been automatically split and converted into a catalog and site manifest

a) A site(.eno) manifest at the root of the site directory (containing site-wide settings)
b) A catalog(.eno) manifest in its own subdirectory (representing the homepage of your site)

In 2.0 you can also make artist, multitrack and track pages the homepage – this
might be preferable if (e.g.) you only present a single artist/band or a single release on it.
Note that this is not done automatically by the migration because it's rather complicated
to make that decision correctly on an automated basis – and you as the site owner know best.
In short: Feel free to merge your 'homepage catalog' data/settings into another page and make
that the homepage instead!
